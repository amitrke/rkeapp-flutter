Roorkee.org is the community platform for everyone who has lived in, studied in, or simply loves the historic town of Roorkee, Uttarakhand.

Share posts, photos, and memories with fellow residents. Browse community news, local weather, and upcoming events — all in one place.

Features:
• Community posts with text and photos
• Photo albums
• Local weather forecast
• Community events and holidays
• News about Roorkee
• Secure sign-in with Google or Apple
